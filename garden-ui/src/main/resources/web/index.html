What just happened

    Each card is a simulated device talking to the same broker as the controller. The controller only ever sees sensor says; truth is the simulator's own view. Each species tells the controller what it likes (the green band), so a cactus is watered at 5 % and a fern at 55 %. Overwatering rots roots, frost and heat kill what cannot take them — try live weather from somewhere far away. Turn on breakdowns and things fail on their own: the device notices a dead pump itself; a stuck sensor is caught by the controller from what it sees in the readings; a drifting one is the hard case nobody notices until the plant suffers. Whatever broke, only the technician fixes it.
